Our recommendation

The best AI setup for most small businesses

  • Main AI teammate: Claude. Writing, numbers, quotes, contracts and planning, with scheduled jobs in the cloud on the Pro plan.
  • Free second teammate: Gemini. Tasks in Gemini is free in the US with a personal Google account, and it's great for errands, scheduling, images and video.
  • For hands-off daily routines: add Grok Bot. Lead replies, review requests, invoice reminders and supplier checks that run on their own.
  • If your marketing lives on Facebook and Instagram: add Muse. Posts, Instagram analytics and Meta ads, free for most owners in the US and Canada.

About $20 a month to start, or about $50 a month with Grok Bot added (US prices, November 2026).

#1 overall

Claude (Anthropic)

Best for writing, analysis, spreadsheets, contracts and reports. Cowork, now built into the main Claude app on paid plans, runs scheduled tasks in the cloud even when your computer is off. Claude Duet, Anthropic's proactive agent built for small business owners, is a research preview on a waitlist for US Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers.

#2 overall

Grok Bot (xAI)

Best for jobs that run on their own every day. Bots have their own cloud computer and email address, can make phone calls (US) to businesses and to customers who've agreed to hear from you (no marketing or mass calls), routines that start when an email or form arrives, and ready-made business templates in the Marketplace. Included with The X Subscription (Premium, about $30 a month, and higher tiers).

Grok Bot Bot Marketplace
#3 overall, best value

Gemini (Google)

Best for Gmail, Calendar, Docs and Sheets, plus images and video. Tasks in Gemini (formerly Spark) runs errands, schedules and recurring jobs, and is free in the US, Canada, Australia, India, Japan and South Korea with a personal Google account. It isn't available yet with work or school Google accounts.

For your business email and documents

Google Workspace

If you want professional email at your own domain, shared drives and Gemini built into Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Meet for your team, Google Workspace is Google's business suite. Note that Tasks in Gemini currently needs a personal Google account, so many owners use both.

Try Google Workspace
#4 overall, best for Facebook and Instagram

Muse (Meta)

With Muse for Small Business, Muse connects your Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and Meta ad accounts in a few clicks, plus tools like Shopify, QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Canva, Slack and Stripe. It plans content, analyzes your ads and drafts campaigns, and nothing publishes, sends or spends without your approval. Free for most needs; paid plans from $20 a month add phone calls to US businesses. US and Canada only.

#5 overall

Dots (OpenAI)

An always-on agent inside ChatGPT that works in Slack and Microsoft Teams. Available with ChatGPT Pro and ChatGPT Business plans, including Business Standard in the US since November 2026, and being tested with some Plus users. A good fit for teams that already live in Slack or Teams.
