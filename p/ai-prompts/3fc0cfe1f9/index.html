Get a week of dinners out of what is already in your fridge

Here is what I have: fridge [list], freezer [list], cupboard [list]. I always have salt, pepper, oil, [your other staples]. Plan [number] dinners for [number] people, each taking no more than [minutes] minutes of my active time. Rules: use the most perishable ingredients in the earliest meals; reuse leftovers deliberately and tell me where; do not repeat the same main protein two nights running; assume I can buy at most three extra items and list them as one short shopping list. For each dinner give me the name, the ingredients from my list it uses, and the method in no more than five sentences. Flag anything that needs defrosting the night before.
