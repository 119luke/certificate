Prompt 123

Build something sellable out of a skill you already have

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I want to work out what I could realistically sell based on a skill I already have. The skill is [describe what you do, including the boring parts]. People already ask me for help with [the thing colleagues or friends come to you for]. Ask me five questions first to understand what outcome I actually produce for people. Then give me six sellable things built on that skill — each one small enough to deliver in under two weeks. For each, name who specifically would buy it, what problem it removes for them, the smallest version I could test in a week, and the main reason it might fail. Do not suggest anything that needs a following, paid advertising or a product I have to build first.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
