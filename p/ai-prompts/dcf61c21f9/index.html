Get the honest critique your colleagues are too polite to give

You are [the role of the person who will actually read this — a hiring manager, a skeptical client, a journal reviewer] and you have ten minutes and no obligation to be kind. Read what follows and tell me: the first place you would stop reading and why, the single weakest claim and what is missing to support it, the strongest objection you would raise in the room, and anything that reads as padding. Do not list strengths. Do not rewrite it. Do not invent problems to seem tough — if something genuinely works, say nothing about it. Here is the draft: [paste your draft].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
