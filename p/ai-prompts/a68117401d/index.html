Prompt 064

Turn scattered notes into something you can search later

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here are my raw notes: [paste the notes]. Reorganize them into this exact structure: Title, Date, People involved, Decisions made, Open questions, Next actions with owners, Tags (five to eight plain-language keywords someone might search for later). Use only what is in my notes — if something is unclear or missing, list it under Open questions rather than filling it in. Keep my wording where it is already clear. Output as plain text I can paste straight into my notes app.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
