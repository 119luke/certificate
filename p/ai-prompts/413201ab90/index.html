Prompt 032

Make one photo work as a banner, a square and a story

Photos · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have one photo: [describe it — what's in frame, where the subject sits, how much empty space is around them]. I need it to work as [list the sizes you need, e.g. a 1500x500 header, a 1:1 square post and a 9:16 story]. For each size, tell me exactly where to crop and where the subject should sit, which parts I'll lose, and where the safe empty space is for a headline. Flag any size this photo genuinely cannot do well and say what I'd need instead. My text overlay will say: [your headline].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
