Prompt 115

Find the words for a boundary you need to set

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me find the words for a boundary. The behavior: [what keeps happening]. What I want instead: [the specific change]. Who it is: [relationship, and how much warmth it needs]. Give me three versions of what to say — one gentle, one plain, one firm — each under four sentences, each stating the boundary once without over-explaining or apologizing. Then give me a one-line reply for each of the two most likely pushbacks, and tell me which sentence in each version is doing the real work so I can keep it if I rewrite.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
