Prompt 116

Get moving on the thing you cannot make yourself start

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I cannot make myself start this: [the task]. It has been sitting there for [how long]. Here is what I think is going on: [anything you know about why]. Do not give me a plan or a step-by-step breakdown. Give me exactly one action — under two minutes, no decisions required, doable right now from where I am sitting — that counts as starting. Then tell me, in two sentences, what I would have to believe about this task for it to feel impossible, and ask me one question that tests whether that belief is true.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
