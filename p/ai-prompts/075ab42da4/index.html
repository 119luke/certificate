Prompt 034

Write a video prompt that gets the shot you actually pictured

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write me a single-shot video prompt, as one flowing paragraph of about 60 words, for a clip of: [subject] doing [one single action]. Setting: [place and time of day]. Framing: [wide / medium / close-up] on [what fills the frame]. Lighting and mood: [e.g. low winter sun, quiet and unhurried]. Look: [e.g. shot on 35mm film, shallow depth of field]. Describe only what a camera could see, keep it to one continuous action with no cuts, phrase everything positively with no "no" or "without", and end with the sound or silence of the scene. Give me three variations that change only the framing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
