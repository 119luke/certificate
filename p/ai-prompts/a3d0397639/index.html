Prompt 092

Prepare the questions to ask in a viva or a demo

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am examining a [viva / demo / practical assessment] for [subject and level]. Here is the brief students worked to: [paste the brief]. Here is the submitted work, with identifying details removed: [paste or describe the work]. Write me 12 questions in four groups of three: recall of what they did, justification of why they chose that approach over an alternative, what happens if a key assumption or input changes, and what they would do differently with more time. For each, give the answer a strong candidate would give and the weak answer that sounds right but is not. Mark any question where you are unsure of the correct answer.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
