Swap an ugly background without wrecking the person in front of it

Replace only the background in this photo. Keep the person completely unchanged — same face, hair, clothing, pose, body edges, and the same direction and softness of light falling on them. Do not relight or recolor them. New background: [a quiet café interior], out of focus with shallow depth of field, lit from [the left] to match the light already on the subject, with the subject standing roughly [two meters] in front of it. Blend the edges naturally, especially around hair and [glasses], with no halo, no hard cut-out line and no color fringing. Match the grain and color temperature of the original photo.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
