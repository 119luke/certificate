Prompt 056

Turn a to-do list into a plan that survives your actual day

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my task list for today. [paste the list, with your own time estimate next to anything you can estimate] My working hours are [start] to [end]. Fixed commitments I cannot move: [meetings, school run, etc. with times]. I am sharpest [morning / afternoon] and my day gets interrupted [rarely / constantly]. Build a realistic plan: total the time my tasks need, compare it to the hours I actually have, and tell me the gap first. Leave at least 25 percent of the day unscheduled for interruptions and overrun. Put demanding work in my sharp hours and admin in the low ones. Then give me two lists: what I am doing today, and what I am explicitly not doing today with a suggested day for each. If something has a hard deadline I'll miss, say so at the top.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
