Prompt 110

Look again at the thought that keeps coming back

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I keep having this thought: "[write the thought as one sentence, in the words your head actually uses]". It usually turns up when [when or where]. Help me look at it on paper. Ask me, one question at a time: what exactly it is claiming, what evidence I would point to for it, what evidence sits against it, what I would say if someone I liked said this about themselves, and what it is costing me to carry. Then help me write a version of the sentence that is fairer but that I could still honestly agree with. Do not tell me the thought is false and do not offer reassurance.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
