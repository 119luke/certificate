Prompt 088

Turn a topic into a lesson that fits the time you have

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Plan a [number]-minute session on [topic] for [number] learners [describe who they are and their level]. Constraints: [room, equipment, internet, anything they must leave able to do]. Give me a minute-by-minute run sheet with the timing for each segment, what I am doing and what the learners are doing in each one, and at least [number] points where they do something active rather than listen. Tell me what to cut first if I lose ten minutes, and what the session should end with so I know whether it worked. If the topic does not fit the time, say so and tell me what to leave out.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
