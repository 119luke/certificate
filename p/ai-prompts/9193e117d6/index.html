Prompt 083

Be asked the questions your examiner will ask

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am defending this work in a [viva / project panel / client review] with [describe who will be in the room]. Here is my summary, method and findings: [paste them]. Act as a skeptical examiner. Give me fifteen questions you would ask, ordered from routine to the ones I would least like to be asked. For each, say in one line why an examiner asks it and what a weak answer sounds like. Include at least three questions about choices I made that I have not justified in the text, and two about what my results do not show.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
