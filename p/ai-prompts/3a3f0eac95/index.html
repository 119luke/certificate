Walk into a medical appointment with the right questions

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have an appointment with [type of clinician] about [situation, in your own words]. What I have noticed: [symptoms or changes, when they started, what makes them better or worse]. What I want from the appointment: [an explanation / a referral / to understand my options / something else]. Do not suggest what this might be and do not give me any medical advice. Instead, help me prepare: write the two-sentence summary I should open with, then a prioritized list of questions to ask, grouped into understanding the problem, what the options are and their trade-offs, what happens next, and what warning signs should bring me back sooner. Keep it to one page I can hold.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
