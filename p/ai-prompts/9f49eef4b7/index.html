Prompt 063

Get a specific answer by briefing it like a contractor

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need [the specific thing you want produced]. It is for [who reads or uses it] and it needs to [what it must achieve]. Constraints: [length, format, tone, budget, deadline, anything banned]. It is finished when [what done looks like]. Before you write it, tell me in three lines how you have understood the job. Then produce it. If anything in my brief is contradictory or missing, say so instead of guessing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
