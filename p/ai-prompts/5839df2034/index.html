Make your product look like it was shot in a studio

Restage this product photo as a studio shot. Keep the product itself completely unchanged — identical shape, proportions, materials, colors, label artwork and every letter of the text on it. Do not redesign or re-render the packaging. Place it on [a matte white surface] against [a seamless light gray background] with a soft gradient. Light it with a large softbox above and slightly front-left, a white bounce card on the right to open the shadows, and a soft natural shadow and faint reflection beneath the product. Sharp focus throughout, [straight-on eye-level] angle, generous empty space [above] for text. Photographic realism, not a 3D render.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
