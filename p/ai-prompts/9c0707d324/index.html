Prompt 117

Ask which of two ideas is stronger, not whether one is good

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have two ideas and I want them compared, not praised. Idea A: [describe it in two or three sentences]. Idea B: [same]. My situation: [time available per week, skills, budget, who you can already reach]. Score both against: how quickly I could test it for little money, how hard it is for someone else to copy, whether people already pay for something like it, how much of it depends on me personally, and what it costs me if it fails. Then commit to a recommendation and give me the three reasons. Finally, tell me the single fact that, if it turned out to be false, would flip your answer.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
