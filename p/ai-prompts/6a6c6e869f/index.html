Prompt 145

Finally make the decision you keep putting off

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have been putting off this decision for [how long]: [describe the decision and the two or three options]. What matters to me in the outcome, in rough order: [list what you actually care about — money, time, risk, relationships, how it feels in a year]. Help me decide. First, tell me what you think I am actually avoiding, based on what I wrote. Then score each option against my criteria and show your reasoning. Then tell me what happens by default if I keep not deciding, and what the cheapest reversible version of each option would be. Finish with the single question I need to answer to unlock this.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
