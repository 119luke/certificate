Prompt 135

Spot what changed between two versions of the same agreement

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Below are two versions of the same agreement. VERSION A (the one I had): [paste version A]. VERSION B (the one I was sent back): [paste version B]. Compare them clause by clause and produce a table with four columns: clause, what changed, whether the change is substantive or just wording, and what practical difference it makes to obligations, money, timing or risk. List every change, including deletions, even small ones. At the end, tell me which three changes deserve the closest look and what I should ask about each. Do not tell me whether to accept the new version.
