Prompt 106

Ask the health question you have been avoiding

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have been putting off asking a health professional about [describe the topic in general terms]. I am not asking you to tell me what is wrong and I do not want a diagnosis. Instead: give me the correct terms for talking about this so I can be precise, explain in general what a clinician would typically want to know about a topic like this, and write me three opening sentences I could say at the start of an appointment — plain, unembarrassing, one of them very short. Then tell me which type of professional usually handles this and how people normally get referred in [country].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
