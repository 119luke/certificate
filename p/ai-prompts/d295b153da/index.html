Prompt 080

Write the report your marker is actually looking for

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here are the marking criteria or assessment requirements: [paste them]. Word limit: [number]. Here is my outline and what I have so far: [paste your plan or draft section headings]. Map my outline against each criterion and tell me which ones I am currently not addressing at all. Suggest a word budget per section based on how the criteria are weighted, and say which section I am probably over-writing. Do not write any of the report for me — I am writing it. Finish with the five questions I should be able to answer in the text if a marker reads it closely.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
