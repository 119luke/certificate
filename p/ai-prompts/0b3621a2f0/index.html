Prompt 138

Cancel something designed to be hard to cancel

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here are the terms of a [subscription, contract, membership] I want to cancel with [company]: [paste the terms, or the cancellation section]. My account reference is [reference] and I started on [date]. First, tell me from these terms only: how cancellation must be made, any notice period, the earliest date it can take effect, and whether any fee or final payment applies. Quote the wording. Second, write me a short cancellation message I can send that states I am canceling, gives my reference, states the effective date under their own terms, instructs them to stop taking payment, and requests written confirmation within [number] days. Mark anything the terms do not actually cover.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
