Prompt 120

Write a proposal that answers the objection before it is raised

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am writing a proposal. Client: [who they are and what they asked for]. What I am proposing: [your approach, timeline, price]. My honest weak points: [e.g. solo operator, no experience in their sector, higher price than likely competitors]. First, list the six things a cautious buyer would privately worry about before signing this, ranked by how likely they are to kill the deal. Then write the proposal — under one page — addressing the top three directly in my own voice, early, without defensiveness. Use only facts I have given you; if you need a proof point I have not supplied, leave a bracketed gap rather than inventing one.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
