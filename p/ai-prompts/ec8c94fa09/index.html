Prompt 065

Write an agenda that keeps the meeting to thirty minutes

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a thirty-minute agenda for a meeting with [number] people about [topic]. We must leave having decided: [decision one], [decision two]. For each item give the outcome we need, the person who leads it, and the minutes allocated, adding up to no more than 28 minutes so there is room to close. Move anything that is information-only into a short pre-read list. If my decisions cannot realistically fit in thirty minutes, say which one should be dropped to another meeting and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
