Prompt 091

Mark a stack of work without giving everyone the same comment

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my marking rubric for [assignment name]: [paste the rubric with criteria and bands]. Below is one anonymised submission: [paste the work]. Draft feedback for this specific piece. For each rubric criterion, quote the exact sentence, calculation or section that shows where this student sits, then write one comment naming what they did and one naming the single most useful improvement. Do not suggest a grade. Do not use generic phrases like 'good effort' or 'needs more detail' — every comment must point at something in this text. Flag anything you are unsure about rather than guessing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
