Get a training plan built around the time you actually have

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Build me a one-week training plan around these real constraints. Goal: [what you want, e.g. run 5k without stopping / get stronger for lifting my kids]. Time I genuinely have: [list the days and minutes, e.g. Tue 30 min early, Thu 45 min, Sun 1 hour]. Equipment and space: [what you actually have]. Current activity level: [honest description]. Things I must work around: [tiredness, shift work, joints, anything]. Lay out each session with what to do and how long. For each one, give me a 15-minute cut-down version for when the day falls apart, and tell me which session to protect if I can only do one. Flag anything I should check with a professional first.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
