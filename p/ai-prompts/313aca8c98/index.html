Here is an email thread I have not read. [paste the whole thread, including quoted replies] Give me, in this order: (1) the decisions that were actually made, each with the sentence and sender the decision rests on; (2) questions raised that nobody answered; (3) every action item with owner and deadline, and write 'no deadline stated' where there isn't one; (4) anything specifically waiting on me, and (5) the current state of play in three sentences. If people disagreed, say who held which position rather than blending them. Do not infer agreement that was not stated in writing. Flag anything ambiguous instead of resolving it.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
