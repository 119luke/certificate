Prompt 013

Steal a proven solution from an industry that is not yours

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

The job I am trying to do is: [describe the underlying job, not your industry — e.g. 'getting a nervous first-time customer to feel confident within five minutes']. I work in [your field]. Name six industries that have solved this same underlying job well and are as far from my field as possible. For each: what exactly they do, why it works mechanically, and a concrete version of it I could run in [your setting] within a month. Do not suggest anything my industry already does. Flag any example where you are unsure the practice is real.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
