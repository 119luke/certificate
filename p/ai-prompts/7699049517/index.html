Prompt 099

Build a wind-down routine out of the evening you actually have

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me build a realistic wind-down routine. My actual evening: [describe it hour by hour — what time you get in, what has to be done, who else is around, when the last work message usually lands]. I want to be asleep by around [time]. What I currently do in the last hour: [be honest, including screens]. Design a sequence that fits in the time I genuinely have, in order, with rough minutes for each part. Do not assume I have a free evening or a spare room. Give me a 10-minute emergency version for nights when everything overruns, and name the one habit in my current evening most likely to be keeping me awake, with the reason.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
