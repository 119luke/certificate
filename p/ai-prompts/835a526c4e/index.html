Know exactly what a lease commits you to

Here is a lease agreement for a property in [city and country]: [paste the agreement]. Do not give me legal advice or tell me whether to sign. Instead, list in plain English: (1) every payment I am committing to, with the amount and the date it falls due; (2) everything I need the landlord's written permission for; (3) every condition that could cost me money at the end of the lease, including deposit deductions; (4) the notice period each side must give and how notice must be delivered; (5) anything that is left vague, undefined or open-ended. Finish with five questions I should ask the landlord or agent before signing.
