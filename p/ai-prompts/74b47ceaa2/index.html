Prompt 036

Turn one photograph into a moving shot

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I'm animating a still photo. The photo shows: [describe exactly what's in it, front to back]. Write me an image-to-video prompt of about 40 words that: applies one camera move only — [e.g. a very slow push in on the subject's face]; animates only [the one or two things that should move, e.g. steam from the cup and a slight shift of the light]; and states clearly that everything else stays completely still with no change to faces, posture or background geometry. Target length [3-5] seconds. Describe motion only — do not describe anything that is not already in the photo.
