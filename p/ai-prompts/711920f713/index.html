Prompt 011

Get a better prompt than the one you were about to type

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a rough request I was about to send you: [paste your rough prompt]. Do not answer it yet. First, rewrite it as a much stronger prompt — one that specifies the audience, the output format, the length, the tone and any constraints a good answer would need. Then list, in one line each, what you added and why it improves the result. Finally, ask me up to three questions you still need answered before you could give me a genuinely useful reply.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
