Prompt 061

Get asked the hard interview questions before your interview

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

You are interviewing me for this role. Here is the job ad: [paste the ad]. Here is my background: [paste your resume or a short summary]. Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer before asking the next. Start easy, then push into the weak points you can see in my background — gaps, short stints, missing skills, anything I would rather not be asked. After each of my answers, tell me in two lines what an interviewer would take from it and what I left out. Do not write my answers for me.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
