Prompt 101

Understand a medical term well enough to ask about it

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Explain the medical term [paste the exact term as it appears] to me as if I have no medical training. Cover: what it literally means, what part of the body or process it refers to, the everyday words patients and clinicians commonly use for the same thing, and whether the term is used differently in different specialties. Then list six questions a patient could sensibly ask a doctor about this term — questions about what it means in general, not about my case. Do not guess at my situation, do not suggest treatments, and tell me plainly if the term is vague or used loosely.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
