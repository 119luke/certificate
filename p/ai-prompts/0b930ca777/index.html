Prompt 003

Ask the thing you are too embarrassed to ask a person

Start Here: 10 Prompts to Try Tonight · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I want to understand [the thing you do not understand] and I am starting from zero. Assume I know none of the background and none of the jargon. Explain it in plain language in under 200 words, then give me one concrete everyday example of it in action. After that, list the three questions someone who does not understand this usually does not know to ask, and answer them briefly. Finally, tell me plainly which parts of your answer depend on my country or my specific situation, and what I would need to check to be sure.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
