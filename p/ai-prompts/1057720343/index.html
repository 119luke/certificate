Get a study plan that fits the date of the exam

Learning Anything Faster · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have an exam or assessment on [date]. Today is [today's date]. The topics are: [paste the syllabus or topic list]. I can realistically study [number] hours on weekdays and [number] hours at weekends, and I am already [strong/weak] on [topic]. Build me a week-by-week plan working backwards from the exam date. Front-load new material, schedule spaced repeats of anything I said I was weak on, and keep the final [number] days for practice questions and review only. Tell me plainly which topics I am giving less time to and what the risk of that is. Then give me just this week in detail, day by day.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
