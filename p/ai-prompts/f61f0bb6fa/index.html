Prompt 144

Turn “it's broken” into a report someone can act on

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Turn this into a fault report someone can act on without replying to ask me questions. What happened: [describe it in your own words, however messy]. Structure it as: one-line summary, what I expected to happen, what actually happened, exact steps to reproduce, how often it happens, when it started, what I have already tried, and the impact on my work. Use only the facts I gave you. Where something important is missing, do not invent it — list it at the end as a question for me to answer. Keep it under 200 words and write it plainly, no apologizing and no padding.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
