Prompt 108

Get a worry out of your head and onto the page

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Act as a structured journalling companion while I write out a worry. The worry is about [one line]. Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer before the next. Work through: what specifically I am afraid will happen, what it would actually look like on the day, how likely I honestly think it is, what is inside my control and what is not, and what the smallest next action would be. Do not reassure me, do not tell me it will be fine, and do not offer advice unless I ask. At the end, reflect back what I wrote in my own words in under a hundred.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
