Prompt 102

Turn “get fitter” into something you can actually measure

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I want to turn a vague fitness goal into something measurable. My goal is [describe it in your own words, e.g. "get fitter"]. Right now I do [describe your current activity honestly]. I realistically have [number] sessions a week of about [number] minutes, and I need to work around [constraint, e.g. no gym, bad knees, small children]. Give me three candidate targets at different levels of ambition. For each: the exact number, the frequency, a review date, how I would track it with no special equipment, and what the first week looks like. Then tell me which one you would expect me to still be doing in eight weeks, and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
