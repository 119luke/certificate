Prompt 021

Turn a phone selfie into a headshot you would put on LinkedIn

Photos · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Using the photo I have uploaded, produce a professional headshot. Keep my face exactly as it is — same bone structure, same nose, same eyes, same skin texture, same age, same hairline. Do not slim, smooth or beautify anything. Change only these: light me with soft diffused light from the front left with a gentle fill on the right, put me against a plain [mid-gray] background with shallow depth of field, and dress me in [a navy crew-neck jumper]. Frame head and shoulders, eyes on the upper third, natural expression. Output at high resolution, suitable for a [LinkedIn] profile picture.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
