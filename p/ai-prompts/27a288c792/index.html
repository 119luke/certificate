Prompt 146

Know what you will do when the plan goes wrong

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my plan: [paste the plan or describe it, with dates]. I want a contingency plan, not a risk register. Identify the five most likely ways this goes wrong in practice. For each, write it as: the trigger (the specific, observable thing that tells me it has happened), my response (the exact fallback action), who I need to tell and by when, and what it costs me. Then give me one abandon condition — the point at which the sensible move is to stop rather than push on. Keep every response to something I could actually do with [your resources: budget, people, time].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
