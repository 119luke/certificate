Prompt 015

Find the idea already buried in your own notes

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Below are my raw, unedited notes from the past few months. [Paste the notes.] Do not summarize them. Instead: list every theme that appears more than once, quoting the lines that show it. Point out where I contradict myself. Identify three pairs of unrelated notes that would make an interesting idea if combined, and say what that combined idea is. Finally, name the one thing I keep circling back to without ever committing to, and quote my own words as evidence.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
