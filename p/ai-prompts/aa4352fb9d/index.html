Prompt 149

Compare two products without reading forty reviews

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am choosing between [product A] and [product B] for [what you will actually use it for, and the constraint that matters most]. Compare them on the things that affect me, not the full spec sheet. Show me: where they genuinely differ, where the difference is too small to matter in practice, the usual complaint owners make about each, and which one suits my use. Then list every fact you are unsure about or that may have changed — prices, current versions, availability — and tell me exactly what to check and where. End with the one question that decides it for someone in my position.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
