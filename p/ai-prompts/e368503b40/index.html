Prompt 127

Chase an unpaid invoice without losing the client

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a message chasing an unpaid invoice. Client: [name and relationship — long-standing, new, referred]. Invoice for [work], amount [amount], due [date], now [number] days overdue. This is chase number [1, 2 or 3]. Previous contact: [what you have already sent and any reply]. I want to keep working with them. Keep it under 130 words, friendly but unambiguous, state the facts once, make one specific ask with a date, and do not apologize for asking. Offer no discount and no new payment terms unless I say so. Do not include any claim about interest, legal rights or consequences — leave a clearly marked gap where I can add my own terms.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
