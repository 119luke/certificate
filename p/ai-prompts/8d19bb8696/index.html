Prompt 082

Turn a topic into a presentation you can stand up and give

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have to give a [number]-minute talk to [describe the audience and their level] on [your topic]. Here is the material it is based on: [paste your report, notes or findings]. Plan the talk slide by slide. For each slide give me a title, the three or four points I make on it, and roughly how many seconds I spend there — the total must fit [number] minutes at a normal speaking pace. Tell me what to cut from my material and why. Mark the one slide that carries the main argument, and give me a closing line that is not a summary.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
