Prompt 087

Explain the same concept three completely different ways

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Explain [concept] to [describe the learners and what they already know] in three completely different ways: one everyday analogy, one fully worked concrete example with real numbers or a real case, and one explanation built around what the learner should picture or sketch. Do not paraphrase the same explanation three times. For the analogy, tell me exactly where it breaks down and what misconception it could create. End with the one prerequisite idea a learner must already have, or none of this will land.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
