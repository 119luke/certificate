Prompt 095

Write a reference letter that says something specific

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am writing a reference for a [student / trainee / colleague] applying for [role or program]. Here is what I actually know about them, with names removed: [paste your rough notes — grades, projects, specific incidents, how long and in what capacity you taught or supervised them]. Draft a reference letter of about [length] aimed at [who will read it]. Every positive claim must be supported by one concrete example from my notes. Do not add any detail I have not given you — if a section needs evidence I have not supplied, write [NEED EXAMPLE] and say what kind. Avoid stock phrases like 'a pleasure to teach' and 'a valuable asset'. Tell me at the end which claims are strongest and which sound thin.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
