Prompt 038

Demo your product without owning a camera

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

My product is [what it is] and it helps [who] do [what] instead of [the painful way they do it now]. Plan a [30 / 60]-second demo video as a shot list. For each shot give me: what's on screen, how long it runs in seconds, and the one line of voice-over. Every shot must show a problem being removed, not a feature being named. Tell me which shots need real footage of the actual product and which could be stock, screen recording or generated. Use only these facts: [list what your product genuinely does]. Do not add any capability I have not listed.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
