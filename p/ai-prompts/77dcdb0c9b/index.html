Prompt 022

Put color into a black-and-white photo without it looking fake

Photos · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Colorise this black-and-white photograph, taken around [1952] in [northern England]. Use a restrained, slightly desaturated palette that matches the film stock of the period — muted earth tones, soft skin tones with natural redness at the cheeks and ears, no orange or plastic-looking skin. Keep the grain, the contrast and every detail of the original exactly as they are; do not sharpen, upscale or retouch. Choose clothing and material colors that were realistic for the period and place. Known details to honor: [the woman's coat was dark green]. Where you are unsure of a color, choose the most neutral plausible option rather than the most vivid.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
