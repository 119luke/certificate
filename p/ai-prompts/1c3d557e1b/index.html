Prompt 124

Get twenty different angles for selling the same thing

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Give me twenty different angles for selling the same thing. What I sell: [describe it plainly]. Who buys it: [your buyers]. What it actually changes for them: [the outcome]. Each angle should be one line and lead with a different motivation — saving time, avoiding an embarrassment, status, fear of getting it wrong, curiosity, money saved, ease, speed, identity, peace of mind. Do not repeat the same idea in different words. After the twenty, group them into three clusters and tell me which cluster is most likely to reach someone who does not yet know they have this problem, and why. Mark any angle that claims a result I would have to prove.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
