Prompt 068

Be taught a subject instead of lectured at

Learning Anything Faster · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Teach me [subject], starting from [what I already know — assume nothing beyond this]. Rules: explain one idea at a time in no more than 150 words, then ask me one question that checks whether I actually got it, then stop and wait for my answer. If my answer is wrong or vague, do not just give me the answer — ask a simpler question that gets me there. If I get it right, move on. We have about [number] minutes. Do not give me the whole topic at once.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
