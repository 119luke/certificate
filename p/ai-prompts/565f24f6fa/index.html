Prompt 114

Review your week in a way that is not just a task list

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my week, in no particular order: [dump everything — what you did, what you avoided, how you felt on which days, anything that happened]. Write me a review in five short parts: (1) what this week was actually about, in one sentence; (2) where my energy went, as opposed to where my time went; (3) the thing I kept putting off, and what my own description suggests is behind it; (4) one moment worth keeping that I would otherwise forget; (5) the single question I should sit with before next week. Be direct. Do not congratulate me and do not give me a productivity system.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
