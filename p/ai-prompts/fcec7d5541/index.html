Prompt 060

Write a job ad or an application that does not sound generic

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Delete whichever of the two bracketed branches below does not apply, then send this. Write a [job ad for the role of X — OR — application letter for the role below]. Here are the real details: [for a job ad: what the person will actually do in their first three months, the tools and systems used, team size, who they report to, salary range and location or remote policy, and the one thing that is genuinely hard about the job]. [for an application: paste the job ad, then your relevant experience with specific projects, numbers and outcomes]. Banned phrases: fast-paced, dynamic, passionate, self-starter, rockstar, wear many hats, detail-oriented, team player, excellent communication skills, results-driven. Every claim must be tied to something concrete from what I gave you. Do not invent any experience, metric, benefit or responsibility I have not stated — leave a bracketed gap instead. Under 400 words, plain English, and tell me afterwards which claims look weakest and what detail would strengthen each.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
