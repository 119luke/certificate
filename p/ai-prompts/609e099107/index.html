Prompt 017

Find the gap everyone in your field keeps stepping over

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I work in [your field] as [your role]. The things people in it complain about constantly but never fix are: [list two or three]. The standard tools or methods are [name them]. Identify five things my field has simply learned to tolerate — inefficiencies, annoyances or bad experiences everyone treats as normal. For each, explain why it has stayed unfixed, whether the reason is a real barrier or just habit, and what a first attempt at fixing it might look like. Be blunt about which of the five are probably unfixable and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
