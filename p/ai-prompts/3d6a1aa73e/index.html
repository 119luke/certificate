Prompt 054

Write the reply you have been avoiding for three days

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have not replied to this for [number] days and I need to now. [paste the message] My relationship with the sender: [colleague / client / my manager / supplier]. What I actually need to say: [the real answer, in rough words — including that it's a no, or that I don't know yet, if that's the case]. Write the reply. Acknowledge the delay in one short clause at most, with no extended apology and no excuse unless I've given you one. Lead with the answer, then the reason, then what happens next with a date. Match a [warm and direct / brisk and professional] tone, keep it under 150 words, and give me a second version that is two sentences long in case I want to send it right now.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
