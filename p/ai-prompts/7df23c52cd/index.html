Prompt 031

Turn a screenshot into a diagram people can follow

Photos · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I'm attaching a screenshot of [what the screen shows, e.g. our expense claim form / the admin settings page]. My audience is [who will follow this, e.g. new starters in their first week]. Read the screenshot and turn it into a step-by-step flow they could follow without seeing the screen: name each step as an action in plain language, mark any point where the user has to make a choice and say what each branch leads to, and list anything the screenshot implies but does not show. If something is cut off or ambiguous, say so instead of guessing. Then give me the same flow written as a simple diagram description I can hand to a designer or an image generator.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
