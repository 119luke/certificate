Prompt 129

Understand what a contract actually says before you sign it

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a contract I have been asked to sign: [paste the text]. I am the [your role — supplier, client, tenant, employee]. Explain it in plain English, clause by clause, in a table with three columns: what the clause says in ordinary words, what it means for me specifically, and how confident you are that you have read it correctly. Then summarize in six bullet-free sentences: what I must do, what they must do, how it ends, what it costs, what happens if either side fails to perform, and what is notably absent. Do not tell me whether to sign and do not comment on whether anything is legally enforceable. Flag every part you are unsure about.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
