Prompt 140

Find the actual cause instead of treating the symptom

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I keep having this problem: [describe the problem concretely]. It started [when], it happens [how often, under what conditions], and these related things are working normally: [list them]. What I have already tried: [list]. Work backwards from the symptom to possible root causes: state the most likely immediate cause, then ask why that happens, and keep going for at least five levels, branching where more than one explanation is plausible. Keep every cause to something I could check or observe, not a guess about motives. Then give me a short list of the cheapest tests that would tell me which branch is the real one, ordered by how quickly I could do them. Say which causes you cannot assess from what I have told you.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
