Prompt 142

Find out what you are underestimating about your own plan

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my plan for [the project or goal], including my timings and assumptions: [paste the plan]. Do not tell me it is a good plan. Tell me what I am most likely underestimating. Go through it looking specifically for: steps I have written as one step that are really three, things that depend on someone else saying yes, time I have not allowed for review, rework or handover, and costs that only appear once the work starts. List the five biggest gaps, each with the question I should go and ask a real person to find out whether it applies to me.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
