Prompt 122

Write a cold message that gets a reply instead of a delete

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a cold outreach message to [name and role] at [company]. Here is the real, specific thing I noticed about their work: [paste the detail — a post, a launch, a problem they mentioned]. Here is what I do: [one sentence]. Rules: under 120 words, no flattery, no 'I hope this finds you well', no paragraph about my passion. Open with the specific detail, one sentence on why that makes me relevant, then one ask that takes them under two minutes to answer. Do not invent any fact about them that I have not given you. Give me three versions with different opening lines and a subject line for each, under seven words.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
