Prompt 090

Write feedback a student will actually act on

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my rubric: [paste the rubric]. Here is an anonymised piece of work: [paste it with all names and identifying details removed]. Draft feedback of no more than [number] words addressed to the learner. Start with what they did well, quoting the specific place they did it. Then give the two or three changes that would most improve their next piece, each with one concrete action they can take. Do not award a mark or a grade. Where the work sits close to a boundary in my rubric, tell me what I should look at before I decide.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
