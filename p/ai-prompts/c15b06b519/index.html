Prompt 050

Turn a podcast episode into show notes with timestamps

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a timestamped transcript of a podcast episode: [paste the transcript]. The show is [show name] and the guest is [name and what they do]. Produce show notes containing: a 60-word episode description written to make someone press play; chapter markers with timestamps placed where the topic genuinely changes, not at even intervals, each with a short descriptive title; five pull quotes with their timestamps; and a list of every book, person, tool or website mentioned, with the timestamp where it comes up. Mark anything you could not identify clearly rather than guessing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
