Prompt 043

Turn a blog post into a script you can actually film

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a blog post I wrote: [paste the post]. Turn it into a script for a [length, e.g. 3-minute] video filmed on [a phone, to camera / with screen recording]. Lay it out as two columns: SPOKEN and ON SCREEN. Spoken lines must be short enough to say in one breath and sound like talking, not like reading. Open with the single most surprising or useful point in the post, not with an introduction. You must cut at least a third of the post's content — at the end, list what you cut and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
