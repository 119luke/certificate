First, list every word in my prompt that carries no visual information and say what the model is probably defaulting to for each. Then write three replacement prompts that are genuinely different from one another — not three rewordings — each specifying subject, camera angle, lens, light source and direction, time of day, color palette, materials and textures, background, mood, and one thing to exclude. Keep each under 90 words and tell me in one line how the three differ.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
