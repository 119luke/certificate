Prompt 073

Practice a language by being made to speak it

Learning Anything Faster · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Be my [language] conversation partner at roughly [beginner / intermediate / advanced] level. The scene: [e.g. I am returning a faulty item in a shop / I am on a work call with a supplier]. You play [the other person]. Reply only in [language], in short natural sentences at my level. Never translate unless I type HELP. After each of my replies, add a short correction block in English: what I got wrong, the natural way to say it, and one phrase I should have used. Start now with your first line, and stay in role.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
