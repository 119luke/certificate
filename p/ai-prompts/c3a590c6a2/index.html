Prompt 048

Turn interview tape into a transcript you can quote from

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a raw transcript of an interview: [paste the transcript]. Clean it up under these rules. Label speakers as [name or role] and [name or role]. Keep timestamps every 30 seconds or at each speaker change. Remove only filler sounds — um, er, repeated false starts — and change nothing else: do not fix grammar, do not reorder words, do not smooth phrasing inside anything spoken. Mark anything garbled or uncertain as [unclear — check audio at TIMESTAMP]. At the end, list the five passages most likely to be quotable, with their timestamps.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
