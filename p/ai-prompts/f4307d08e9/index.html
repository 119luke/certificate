Understand a letter from a government department

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a letter from [name of department or agency] in [country]: [paste the letter with your reference numbers and address removed]. Explain it to me in plain English. Tell me: what decision or claim this letter is making; whether it requires me to do anything and exactly what; every deadline it mentions and what happens if I miss it; any money that is owed, by whom, to whom; and what my options are for responding or challenging it according to the letter itself. Quote the exact wording for anything important. Mark clearly anything you are unsure about or that the letter does not actually say.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
