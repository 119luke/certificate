Prompt 051

Write a thirty-second audio ad that people do not skip

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a thirty-second single-voice audio ad for [what you are selling] aimed at [who listens and what they are doing while they listen]. Target 80 spoken words and tell me the word count and estimated read time at natural pace. Open on a moment the listener recognizes from their own week, not on my brand name — hold the brand back until at least halfway. One offer, one action, and repeat the action or the web address once at the end so it can be remembered without writing it down. No two-person dialogue, no rhetorical question openers, no 'in today's world'. Then give me two alternative first lines that take a different angle on the same problem.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
