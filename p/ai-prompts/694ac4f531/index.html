Prompt 049

Turn a rambling voice note into clean, usable notes

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

This is a transcript of a voice note I recorded while [driving / walking / between meetings], so it rambles and doubles back: [paste the transcript]. Reorganize it — do not summarize it — under four headings: Decisions I seem to have made, Actions with any names or dates I mentioned, Open questions, Loose ideas worth keeping. Keep my own phrasing wherever you can. Where I trailed off or contradicted myself, say so and quote the fragment rather than completing the thought. End with anything I said twice, since that is probably what is bothering me most.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
