Prompt 078

Work out which method to use, and be able to defend it

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

My question is: [state it precisely]. My data or materials: [describe size, type, how collected]. Constraints: [time, software, skills, sample size, access]. Compare the three or four methods genuinely open to me here. For each one give: what it assumes, whether my situation actually meets those assumptions, what it would let me claim, and what it would not. Then say which you would argue for and what the strongest objection to that choice is. Do not cite specific papers or authors. Finish with the questions I should take to my supervisor before I commit.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
