Prompt 137

Find out whether you are actually covered before you claim

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my insurance policy wording: [paste the policy document]. Here is what happened: [describe the event, including the date and anything that might be relevant]. Do not tell me whether my claim will succeed. Instead: quote the sections of this policy that appear to apply to this event; quote every exclusion, condition or limit that mentions anything resembling my situation; state the excess and any claim limits; list exactly what the policy says I must do and by when after an event like this; and list what evidence the policy requires. Finish with the questions I should ask the insurer before I submit anything.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
