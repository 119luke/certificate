Prompt 012

Get thirty ideas when you are stuck for one

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need ideas for [describe the thing: a talk on X for Y audience, a birthday present for someone who Z, a way to reduce no-shows at our clinic]. Give me 30 distinctly different ideas. Number them. Each one gets one sentence, no explanation. Do not repeat the same idea in different words. Make ideas 1 to 10 safe and conventional, 11 to 20 unusual but practical, 21 to 30 genuinely odd — including ones I would probably reject. Then pick the three you think are strongest and say in one line why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
