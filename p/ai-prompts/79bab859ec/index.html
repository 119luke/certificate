Dispute a charge and get your money back

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me write a card payment dispute to [bank or card provider]. The facts are: I paid [amount] to [merchant] on [date] for [what it was], and [describe exactly what went wrong and what you have already done to sort it out with the merchant]. Use only these facts and write [MISSING] where you need something I have not given you. Produce: (1) a one-line statement of what the dispute is, in the plainest category you can, such as goods not received or payment taken after cancellation; (2) a factual account in date order under 250 words; (3) a list of the evidence I should attach; (4) the questions I should ask my provider about deadlines and process. Do not invent any dates, amounts or policy rules.
