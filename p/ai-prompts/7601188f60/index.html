Prompt 119

Put a price on your work without guessing

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me price this properly. The work: [what you deliver, and the scope]. My estimate of the hours: [including revisions and admin]. My costs: [tools, subcontractors, anything you pay out]. Minimum I need to earn per working hour to be viable: [amount]. The client: [type of client, what this work is worth to them]. Give me: a floor price I should not go below and the arithmetic behind it; a recommended price and why; a higher version with what I would add to justify it; the three things about my scope that most often cause an underestimate; and one sentence I can say when someone asks for a discount. Flag any figure you are estimating rather than calculating.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
