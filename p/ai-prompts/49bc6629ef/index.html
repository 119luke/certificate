Prompt 039

Write a script that lands in exactly thirty seconds

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a 30-second script about [your subject] for [who it's for], to be spoken by one person straight to camera. Hard limit: 80 words total — count them and show me the count. Structure it as a hook in the first 3 seconds that names the problem, one single idea in the middle, and one specific action at the end. Mark the running time next to each section. Write it the way people talk: short sentences, contractions, no lists, no jargon, nothing I'd be embarrassed to say out loud. Then give me a 60-word version for if I read slowly.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
