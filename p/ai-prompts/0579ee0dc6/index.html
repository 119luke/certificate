Work through a fault one step at a time until it is fixed

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Act as a patient troubleshooter and work through this one step at a time. The problem: [describe the fault, including the exact error message, when it started, and what changed just before]. My setup: [device, software or model, and version if you know it]. Rules: give me ONE thing to check or try, and nothing else. Explain in one line what result would tell you what. Then wait for my answer before suggesting anything further. Start with the cheapest, least destructive test. If you reach the point where this needs a professional or a replacement part, say so plainly instead of continuing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
