Prompt 089

Write a project brief students cannot misread

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a project brief I am about to give to [describe the learners]: [paste the brief]. Read it as a capable but anxious learner who has not been in the room when I explained it. List every sentence that could reasonably be read two different ways, and say what the two readings are. List anything they would have to guess because I have not stated it — deliverable format, word count, whether references count, how group work is credited, what the deadline actually means. Then rewrite the brief so each of those is unambiguous, keeping my structure and my requirements exactly as they are.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
