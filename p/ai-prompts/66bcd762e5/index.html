Prompt 125

Write listing copy that sells the thing you are selling

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a listing for [the item or service] on [where it is being listed]. Facts: [condition, age, size, what is included, price, collection or delivery, and the reason you are selling]. Write a title under 80 characters that leads with what someone would actually search for, then a description of about 150 words in plain language. No hype, no adjectives I have not earned. Do not add any detail I did not give you. After the listing, give me a separate list of the five questions a cautious buyer would still have that my facts do not answer, so I can add them myself.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
