Prompt 070

Be tested on it instead of told you understood it

Learning Anything Faster · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Test me on [topic], at the level of [exam, certification or the standard you need]. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Mix formats: some recall, some applying it to a new situation, and at least one that makes me explain why. After each answer, tell me what I got right, what was missing, and what a full-mark answer would have included. Do not accept a vague answer — push me for the specifics. Bring back anything I get wrong later in the session. Keep going until I say stop.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
