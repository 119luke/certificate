Get a journalling prompt that goes deeper than “how was your day”

Write me eight journalling prompts for tonight. What has been on my mind lately is [one or two sentences]. I want the writing to help me [notice patterns / stop circling something / work out what I actually think]. Rules: no prompt may ask "how was your day" or anything as broad. Each one must point at a single specific moment, sentence or reaction rather than a general theme. Make two of them slightly uncomfortable. Keep each under twenty words, and put the easiest one first so I can start.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
