Here is the assessment brief for my work: [paste the full brief, including any rubric and word count]. Here is my draft: [paste your draft]. Go through the brief requirement by requirement. For each one, quote the requirement, say whether my draft meets it, partly meets it or misses it, and point to the exact sentence or section where it is met. List anything the brief asks for that I have not done at all. Do not comment on quality or style yet — only on whether each stated requirement is satisfied. Do not add requirements that are not in the brief I pasted.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
