Prompt 093

Find out whether a student understands what they handed in

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is an anonymised piece of student work for [subject and level]: [paste the work]. Write me 10 questions I could ask the author in a short conversation about their own process — questions that can only be answered by someone who actually produced this. Focus on decisions and discards: why this source or method rather than an obvious alternative, what they tried that did not work, where they got stuck, what a specific sentence or figure means in their own words. Do not write questions about whether the work is authentic, and do not assess the work. Keep the tone neutral and usable with any student.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
