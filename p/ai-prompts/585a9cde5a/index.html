Prompt 084

Write a question paper with a real spread of difficulty

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Write a [length]-minute assessment on [topic] for [describe the learners and their level]. Cover these syllabus points: [list them]. Give me [number] questions with this spread: 40% straightforward recall or definition, 40% applying the idea to a described situation, 20% that require transferring the idea to a context we have not taught. Label each question with its level and the syllabus point it tests, and give the mark allocation. Flag any question where you are not fully confident the answer is unambiguous.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
