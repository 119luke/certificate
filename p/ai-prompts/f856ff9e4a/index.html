Prompt 045

Turn a long document into something you can listen to

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is a document: [paste the document or the section you need]. Rewrite it as a script to be read aloud, for a listener who cannot see the page. Keep every substantive point but restructure for the ear: open with one sentence on what this covers and how long it runs, signpost each new section in plain speech, spell out abbreviations on first use, round and speak numbers naturally, and remove all references to tables, figures or anything visual. Target [number] minutes of speech at a normal reading pace. Flag anything you had to simplify at the end.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
