Prompt 141

Get three real options with the trade-offs spelled out

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need to decide [the decision], and here is my situation: [budget, deadline, who else is involved, what I have already ruled out]. Give me exactly three genuinely different options, not three versions of the same one. For each, write what it costs in money, time and hassle, what I gain, what I give up, who has to agree to it, and the conditions under which it is the right choice. End by naming which option you would drop first and why. Mark any figure you have estimated rather than known, so I know what to check.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
