Write lyrics for a [genre] song about [specific subject — an actual situation, not a theme]. Structure: verse, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge, chorus. Verse lines should be around [number] syllables, chorus lines around [number], and the rhyme scheme should be [e.g. ABAB in verses, AABB in chorus].
