Prompt 150

Settle an argument, and know when to trust the answer

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Settle this for us. The question: [the disagreement, worded as neutrally as you can]. My position: [yours]. Their position: [theirs]. Answer it, then do three things. One, tell me how confident you are and why — say plainly if this is disputed, out of date, or depends on how a term is defined. Two, name the specific sources or type of source where this is established, and tell me what to search for to confirm it myself. Three, say what each of us got right, and whether we are actually arguing about the same thing. Do not guess to give us a clean winner — if the honest answer is "it depends", say that and explain what it depends on.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
