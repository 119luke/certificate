Prompt 136

Draft a simple agreement for a professional to check

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need a first draft of a simple written agreement to take to a lawyer for review. It is between [party A and what they do] and [party B and what they do], in [country/state]. What we have agreed is: [describe the arrangement, the money, the dates and anything either of us has promised]. Write a plain-English draft covering the parties, what each side must do, payment amounts and dates, how long it lasts, how either side ends it, what happens to [the relevant asset, deposit or work] if it ends early, and how disputes get raised. Use no legal jargon. Where a real contract would normally need a clause I have not mentioned, add it as a bracketed note marked FOR THE LAWYER rather than drafting it.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
