Prompt 113

Prepare yourself for something you are dreading

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I am dreading this: [describe the event, when it is, and what specifically worries you]. Help me prepare rather than cheer me up. Give me: (1) what is actually in my control here and what is not, listed separately and honestly; (2) the three most likely ways it goes, including the realistic dull one; (3) exactly what I do in the first ten minutes — what I bring, where I stand, what I say first; (4) one sentence I can use if I am asked something I cannot answer or I get upset; (5) what I will do straight afterwards. No pep talk, no affirmations, no telling me it will be fine.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
