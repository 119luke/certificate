Prompt 126

Plan a launch you can run on your own without burning out

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me plan a launch I can run alone without burning out. What I am launching: [the thing]. Launch date: [date]. Hours I genuinely have per week, and which days: [be honest]. What already exists: [list what is done]. Work backwards from the launch date to today and give me a week-by-week plan that never exceeds my stated hours. If the work does not fit, cut scope rather than squeezing it, and list plainly what you cut and what I lose by cutting it. Mark the two weeks most likely to overrun and say what I should do if they do. Flag anything that depends on someone other than me.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
