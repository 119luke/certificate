Prompt 014

Turn a tight budget or deadline into sharper ideas

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need to [describe the goal] by [date] with [budget or resources] and it is just [who is actually available to do the work]. Give me eight ideas that fit inside those limits exactly. For each one, state the estimated cost, the hours needed and who does what. Then answer this separately: if my budget were one tenth of that and the deadline were a week sooner, what would I do instead? Tell me which parts of my original plan that version proves I do not actually need.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
