Prompt 076

Plan a project from the brief to the deadline

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my project brief: [paste the brief]. Deadline: [date]. Today is [today's date]. I can work on it [hours per week]. Known fixed dates: [supervisor meetings, approvals, holidays, anything you are waiting on]. Build a plan working backwards from the deadline. Break it into tasks of no more than a few days each, and for each task say what it produces and what cannot start until it is done. Mark every point where I am dependent on someone else and add slack there. Flag anything in the brief that I have not accounted for. Finish with the three earliest warning signs that I am behind schedule.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
