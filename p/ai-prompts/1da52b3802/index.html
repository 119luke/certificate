Prompt 044

Write captions people read all the way to the end

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

My video shows [describe what actually happens in the video, in two or three sentences]. It is going on [platform] and the audience is [who they are]. Write five caption options, each under [number] words. Each one must open with a different kind of first line — a question, a flat claim, a specific number, an admission, a short story opening — and the first line must make sense before the "see more" cut. End each with one clear thing for the viewer to do. Do not add hashtags; list five suggested tags separately and mark any you are not confident are actively used.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
