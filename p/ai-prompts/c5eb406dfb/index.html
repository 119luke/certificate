Prompt 109

Put a name to a feeling you cannot quite describe

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me find the word for something I am feeling. Here is what happened: [describe the situation plainly]. Here is what I notice in my body and behavior: [e.g. tight chest, checking my phone, avoiding the room]. Offer me ten candidate feeling words, from ordinary to unusual. For each, give a one-line definition and the situation it usually fits. Then group the ones that are close together and explain in a sentence how each pair differs. Do not tell me which one is correct — ask me two questions at the end that would help me narrow it down myself.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
