Prompt 055

Turn an hour-long meeting into who does what by when

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is the transcript of a [length] meeting about [subject]. [paste transcript] Extract only the commitments. Give me a table with four columns: action, owner, deadline, and the quote it comes from. Use the exact speaker label from the transcript and never guess a name that isn't there. Then, separately: (1) actions agreed with nobody assigned, (2) actions with an owner but no date, (3) vague commitments like 'look into it' or 'circle back', written out so I can chase them, and (4) decisions that were made but require no action. Ignore discussion that led nowhere. If something is ambiguous, list it under 'needs confirming' rather than deciding what it meant.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
