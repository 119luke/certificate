Build the marking scheme while you write the questions

Here are my assessment questions: [paste the questions with their marks]. For each one, draft a marking scheme: what a full-mark answer must contain, how the marks break down point by point, which alternative wordings or approaches should also get full credit, and the two most likely wrong answers with how much partial credit each deserves. Where a question is too ambiguous to mark consistently, say so and suggest a rewording. I will be checking every answer myself, so tell me which ones you are least confident about.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
