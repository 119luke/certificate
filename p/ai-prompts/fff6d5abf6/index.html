Prompt 118

Find what people are already paying someone else to solve

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Group: [a specific group you can actually reach — e.g. independent physiotherapists in a mid-sized UK city]. Problem area: [the messy job you think they struggle with]. List the ways this group already pays to deal with this today — services, freelancers, software, staff time, DIY workarounds — and for each one, what people typically complain about it. Then name the three gaps where someone paying for the current option is visibly unhappy. Do not invent statistics or prices; where you are unsure, say so and tell me exactly where I could check it myself. Finish with the five questions I should ask one of these people in a fifteen-minute call.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
