Prompt 040

Get the title, the description and the thumbnail line in one go

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

My video is about [topic] and here's what actually happens in it: [two or three sentences of the real content]. Audience: [who]. Give me eight title options under 60 characters each, in different styles — plain and descriptive, a question, a number, and a surprising claim the video genuinely backs up. For each title, give a matching thumbnail line of no more than four words. Then write one description: the first two lines readable on their own as a reason to watch, then a short summary, then chapter-style timestamps marked as placeholders for me to fill in. Flag any title that promises more than my content delivers.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
