Prompt 112

Write the letter you are never going to send

Mind, Mood and Reflection · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have written a letter I am never going to send, to [who it is to]. Do not rewrite it, do not tidy it, and do not write a version of your own. Here it is, exactly as it came out: [paste your letter, however messy]. Read it closely and tell me: what is the strongest feeling actually in this, not the one I say I feel; what am I circling around without saying outright; which sentence is doing the most work; and what I seem to want from this person that I never ask for. Ask me one question at the end that would make the letter more honest if I wrote it again.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
