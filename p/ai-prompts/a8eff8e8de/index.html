Prompt 062

Hear what a twenty-year veteran would say to your face

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Answer as someone with twenty years' experience in [your field or trade], speaking privately to a competent newcomer. Here is my situation: [describe it in five lines]. Tell me what you would say to my face: what I am getting wrong, what I am worrying about that does not matter, and the thing that goes wrong at this stage that nobody warns people about. Be direct, skip the encouragement, and flag anything you are unsure about rather than smoothing it over.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
