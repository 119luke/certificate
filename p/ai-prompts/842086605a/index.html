Prompt 148

Work out whether to repair it or replace it

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me decide whether to repair or replace [the item, with age, model and rough condition]. The fault: [what is wrong]. Quoted repair cost: [amount, or "not yet quoted"]. A replacement costs about [amount]. Here is its history: [previous repairs, how heavily it is used, how long I need it to last]. Work out the cost per year of each option over the time I need, factor in running costs and likely further faults at this age, and tell me which way the numbers point and by how much. Then tell me what single piece of information would change your answer, and mark anything you estimated rather than knew.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
