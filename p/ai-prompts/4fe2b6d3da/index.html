Prompt 058

Pull the five things that matter out of a sixty-page report

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need to get the five things that matter to me out of this document. [attach or paste the report] My role: [your role]. The decision or task in front of me: [what you actually have to do with this]. Give me the five points that most affect that decision, ranked by how much they affect it, each in two sentences with the page or section number it comes from. Quote any figure exactly as written. Then list: anything in here that contradicts something else in the document, anything stated as a conclusion without evidence behind it, and the three pages I should read in full myself. If the document does not actually address my decision, say so plainly instead of finding something.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
