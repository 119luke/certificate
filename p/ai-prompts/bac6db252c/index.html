Prompt 132

Walk into a lawyer's office with the right questions ready

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have a meeting with a lawyer about [describe the situation in a few sentences] in [country/state]. Help me prepare, not advise me. First, turn what I have told you into a one-page factual summary in date order, marking anything I have left unclear. Second, list the documents and records I should bring. Third, write twelve questions to ask in the meeting, ordered most important first, including questions about likely cost, likely timescale, and what happens if I do nothing. Fourth, list what I should ask the lawyer to put in writing afterwards. Do not tell me what the law says or what my chances are.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
