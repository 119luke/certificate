Prompt 077

Get code you can explain, not just code that runs

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is code for [what it does]: [paste your code, or describe exactly what you need]. Explain it to me line by line as if I will be questioned on it. For each meaningful choice — data structure, loop, library, error handling — say why it is there and what the obvious alternative would be, plus why you did not use it. Then list the three questions an examiner or reviewer is most likely to ask me about this code, and wait for my answers before telling me whether they are right. Finally, point out any part of it I should test myself because you might be wrong.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
