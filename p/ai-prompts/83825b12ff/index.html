Find the clauses in an agreement that deserve a closer look

Contracts, Letters and Legal Paperwork · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is an agreement: [paste the text]. I am the [your role]. Go through it and tell me whether it contains, and what it says about, each of the following: how and when either side can end it, notice periods, automatic renewal, exclusivity or non-compete restrictions, who is liable for what and any caps on that, indemnities, who owns the work or intellectual property, confidentiality, payment timing and late payment, changes to terms without my agreement, dispute resolution, and which country's or state's law governs it. For each: quote the exact wording, say what it appears to mean for me, and mark it 'present', 'absent' or 'unclear'. Do not judge whether any of it is fair or enforceable. End with the eight questions I should take to a lawyer.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
