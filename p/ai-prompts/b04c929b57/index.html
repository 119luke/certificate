Prompt 147

Catch a wrong answer by making it show its working

Solving Real Problems · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Work through this and show every step before you give me an answer: [paste the problem, calculation or question]. For each step, write what you are doing, the numbers or facts you are using, and where those came from — my input, general knowledge, or an assumption you are making. List your assumptions separately and flag any you are unsure about. When you reach the answer, do a sanity check: estimate it a second way and tell me whether the two agree. If they do not, say so and tell me which step is most likely wrong rather than picking a number.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
