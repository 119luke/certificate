Prompt 057

Write the status update nobody has to chase you for

Work and Productivity · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Turn these rough notes into a written status update on [project name] for [who reads it — my manager, the client, the wider team]. [paste your raw notes, including anything that has gone wrong] Structure it as: one-line status (on track / at risk / off track) with the reason; what has changed since the last update on [date]; risks and blockers, each with the specific thing I need from the reader and by when; what happens next with dates. Under 200 words, plain sentences, no adjectives about how hard we worked. Do not invent any date, figure or percentage I have not given you — write 'TBC' instead. End with the single decision I need from them, if there is one.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
