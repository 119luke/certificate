Prompt 018

Name the thing you are building so people remember it

Generating New Ideas · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I need a name for [what it is and what it does] aimed at [who uses it]. It will be [said out loud on calls / printed on packaging / typed into a search bar]. The feeling should be [e.g. calm and unfussy], definitely not [e.g. cute or techy]. I have already rejected [names or styles], so avoid those. Give me 20 names in four groups of five: plainly descriptive, an ordinary word used in an unexpected way, two words joined, and borrowed from an unrelated field. One line each on why it works. Do not invent misspelled words, and do not tell me whether any of these are available — I will check that myself.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
