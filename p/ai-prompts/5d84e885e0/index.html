Prompt 079

Present your results without overstating them

Projects and Reports · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here are my results: [paste your figures, table or key findings]. Here is what I claimed about them: [paste your discussion or conclusion]. Act as a skeptical examiner. For every claim I made, tell me whether my results actually support it, overstate it, or need a caveat. Ask me what baseline or comparison I measured against, and if I have not given one, say so. List the three limitations a reader will notice that I have not mentioned, and rewrite my strongest overclaim into a sentence I could defend. Do not invent or recalculate any numbers — work only from what I gave you.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
