Prompt 033

Design a poster or flyer that does not look homemade

I'm making a [poster / flyer / A5 handout] for [the event or thing, with date, place and who it's for]. The single most important line is: [the one thing a passer-by must read]. Secondary information: [the rest]. Give me a design spec, not a description: two typefaces that work together with a free alternative for each, a palette of three or four colors with hex codes, the exact size order of every text element, how much margin to leave, and one focal image idea. Tell me what to cut. Then write the image-generator prompt for the background art only, with space left clear where the text will go.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
