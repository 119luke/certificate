Prompt 128

Work out what the client is really asking you for

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is what a client has asked me for: [paste their message or brief, verbatim]. Context I have: [who they are, what happened before, any budget or deadline]. Give me four different readings of what they might actually be trying to achieve, from the most literal to the least, and for each one say what evidence in their words supports it and what would prove it wrong. Then give me five short questions I could ask them that would tell the readings apart, ordered so the most revealing comes first. Do not assume anything about them that is not in what I gave you — mark clearly where you are guessing.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
