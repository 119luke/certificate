Prompt 009

Rehearse a difficult conversation before you have to have it

Start Here: 10 Prompts to Try Tonight · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Play the part of [who they are to you] in a conversation I have to have. Context: [what has happened]. What I want out of it: [your outcome]. How they usually react when challenged: [defensive, silent, quick to apologize and change nothing]. Stay fully in character, reply in one or two short lines at a time like a real conversation, and do not be unrealistically reasonable. I will open. When I type STOP, drop the role and tell me: where my wording made you defensive, which sentence worked best, and the one question I did not ask that I should have. Here is my opening line: [what you plan to say].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
