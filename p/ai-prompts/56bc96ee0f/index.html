Prompt 086

Make three versions of the same paper so nobody can copy

Teaching, Training and Marking · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is my assessment: [paste the paper, with marks]. Produce three versions, A, B and C, that test exactly the same skills at exactly the same difficulty. Keep the number of questions, the mark allocation and the question types identical. Vary the scenarios, the data, the names and the contexts so the answers differ. Give me a table mapping each question in A to its equivalent in B and C, and note anywhere you think one version may have come out slightly harder or easier and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
