Prompt 052

Write a voice-over that sounds spoken, not written

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Rewrite this script so it sounds like a person speaking, not a document being read. [paste your script] Context: it will be read by [one narrator / me / a professional voice] for [what the video or audio is]. Rules: average sentence under 18 words, one idea per sentence, use contractions, write numbers and units the way a person says them, and replace any word I would not say out loud in conversation. Mark natural pause points with a line break. Do not add filler phrases or fake enthusiasm, and do not change any fact or figure. Keep it within ten percent of the original length. Afterwards, list the three sentences that will still be hardest to read aloud and give me an easier version of each.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
