Prompt 005

Get it right first time by making it ask you questions first

Start Here: 10 Prompts to Try Tonight · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I want you to produce [what you want — a two-page project brief, a resignation letter, a lesson plan]. Before you write a single word of it, ask me the questions you need answered to get it right first time. Ask them one at a time, no more than six in total, and start with the one that would change the output most. Do not guess or fill in gaps yourself — if something matters and I have not told you, ask. When you have enough, say "ready" and give me a one-line summary of what you are about to write, and only write it once I confirm.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
