Prompt 006

Say no to extra work without damaging the relationship

Start Here: 10 Prompts to Try Tonight · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Help me decline this request politely and clearly. The request: [paste or describe what was asked]. Who is asking: [their relationship to me — my manager, a peer in another team, a client]. Why I am saying no: [your real reason]. What I can genuinely offer instead, if anything: [a later date, a smaller piece of it, a better-placed person, or nothing]. Write it as a message under 90 words. Say no in the first two sentences. One reason only, stated as a fact rather than an excuse, and do not invite them to negotiate around it. No more than one apology, and no "unfortunately". Then give me a slightly warmer version and a slightly firmer one.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
