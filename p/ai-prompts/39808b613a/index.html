Prompt 097

Find the three gaps in how you have really been eating

Health, Fitness and Food · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Here is what I actually ate and drank over the last [number] days, including the unglamorous bits: [write it out day by day, with rough portions and times]. Context: [age range, activity level, anything relevant like shift work or cooking for others]. Do not give me a meal plan and do not comment on my weight. Identify the three biggest patterns or gaps in how I am eating, ordered by how much difference fixing them would make. For each one, tell me what you noticed in my log, why it matters in one plain sentence, and one small change that fits the way I already eat. Say clearly which of your observations are confident and which are guesses from limited information.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
