Prompt 121

Turn finished work into a portfolio piece that wins the next job

Making Money · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I want to turn a finished project into a short case study for my portfolio. Here is what I did: [describe the project in a few sentences]. Before you write anything, ask me up to eight questions to fill the gaps — what problem the client had before, why they chose me, what I decided and what I rejected, what got difficult, and what changed afterwards. Then write it in about 300 words under four plain headings: the situation, what I did, why I did it that way, and the result. Use only facts I gave you. Where I could not give you a result, write the sentence as a description of the change rather than a number, and flag it so I can add evidence later. My audience is [type of client I want next].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
