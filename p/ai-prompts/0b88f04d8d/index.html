Prompt 046

Get background music that fits your video's mood and length

Audio, Voice and Music · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

I have a [length] video about [subject]. The mood is [mood] and it shifts at around [timestamp] from [feeling] to [feeling]. Write three different prompts I can paste into an AI music generator to get an instrumental backing track. Each prompt must name specific instruments, give a tempo in BPM, describe the arc over the full [length], and specify that the mix stays sparse in the mid-range so a voice sits on top. Make the three genuinely different in genre, not three versions of the same idea. After each, say in one line what kind of video it would suit best.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
