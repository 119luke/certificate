Prompt 037

Keep the same character in every clip of your video

Video · 150 AI Prompts That Actually Work

Build me a reusable character description for a video series about [what the series is]. The character is [rough sketch: age range, role, vibe]. Write it as one dense block of 50-70 words I can paste unchanged into every clip prompt, covering only fixed physical facts: approximate age, build and height, hair color, length and style, face shape, skin tone, eye color, and one exact outfit including colors and materials. No personality words, no mood words, nothing that could change between scenes. Then show me the block used inside three example shot prompts: [scene one], [scene two], [scene three].

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
