Prompt 016

The follow-up email

Chapter 4: The Freelancer's AI Workflow · The Honest AI Money Guide

I finished [project] for [client name / business] [two weeks / a month] ago. Draft a follow-up email under 120 words that: (1) asks how the work performed, (2) offers one specific, useful tweak or next step based on [what I delivered], (3) does not sound desperate or salesy. Include a subject line. The goal is to restart the conversation naturally, not to pitch.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
