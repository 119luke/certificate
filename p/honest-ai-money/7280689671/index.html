Prompt 012

The proposal drafter

Chapter 4: The Freelancer's AI Workflow · The Honest AI Money Guide

I am a freelance [service, e.g. "blog writer"] specializing in [niche, e.g. "content for roofing companies"]. I found this job posting / client: [paste the posting or describe the business and what they need]. Draft a proposal under 200 words with: (1) a two-sentence opening about THEIR problem, not my background, (2) one specific idea for their business that shows I did my homework, (3) what's included and my turnaround time, (4) a one-line call to action. Tone: confident, plain-spoken, no flattery. Do not use the words "passionate," "leverage," or "cutting-edge."

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
