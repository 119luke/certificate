I am considering using AI to handle the following task: [describe the task]. Act as a risk reviewer. Evaluate: (1) What is the worst realistic outcome if the AI gets this wrong? Who specifically gets hurt, and how badly? (2) Does this involve medical, legal, financial, employment, or other high-stakes advice or decisions about real people? (3) Would I be comfortable signing my name to the AI's output publicly? (4) What guardrails would make this safe — human review steps, verification requirements, or limits on what the AI is allowed to decide? Give me a verdict: automate freely, automate with guardrails (and list them), or do not automate. Be conservative — I would rather you talk me out of something profitable than into something reckless.
