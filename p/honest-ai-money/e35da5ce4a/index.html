Prompt 009

Build your first template

Chapter 3: Prompt Engineering That Actually Earns · The Honest AI Money Guide

I do [describe your main service or deliverable — e.g., "write blog posts for local service businesses"]. Help me turn it into a reusable prompt template. The template needs: 1. Bracketed slots for every detail that changes per client (client name, audience, topic, goal, word count, tone) 2. The five stages: research, outline, draft (one section at a time), critique, polish — with "wait for my approval" gates between stages 3. Constraint sections for audience, tone, length, and format 4. A banned-phrases list I can add to over time 5. A quality checklist the AI must run before delivering the final version Ask me three clarifying questions about my typical deliverables first, then build the template.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
