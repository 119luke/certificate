I need to pick one AI income lane and commit for 90 days. Here is my situation: [describe your skills, job history, and interests in 3-4 sentences]. I can commit [X] hours per week. I need my first income within [timeframe]. My attitude toward direct sales is [love it / tolerate it / avoid it — and if avoid, note I'm open to AI-agent-assisted outreach]. I [have / don't have] an existing audience of roughly [size]. My monthly tool budget beyond basics is $[amount]. The seven lanes are: (1) freelancing with AI, (2) content business, (3) digital products, (4) AI services for local businesses, (5) print-on-demand, (6) paid newsletters, (7) micro-SaaS/AI wrappers. Score each lane 1-10 for my fit. For each, name the biggest advantage I'd have and the single hardest part for someone like me. Then recommend exactly ONE lane, explain why in three sentences, and give me my first three moves this week. Be direct — don't hedge with "it depends."
