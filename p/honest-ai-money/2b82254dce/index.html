Help me prepare for a salary negotiation. My current salary is [amount], my role is [title], and I've been here [duration]. Here are my key accomplishments this year: [paste 3-5 items from your wins file]. Here is what I've found about market pay for my role: [paste what you found from job postings or salary sites]. Draft: (1) a 60-second opening statement for the conversation, (2) responses to the three most likely pushbacks ("budget is tight," "let's revisit next year," "that's above our band"), and (3) three questions I should ask if the answer is no, so I leave with a path forward instead of just a rejection. Keep the tone confident but collaborative.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
