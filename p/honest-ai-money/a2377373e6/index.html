Prompt 017

Content calendar builder

Chapter 5: The Content Business · The Honest AI Money Guide

I run a [newsletter / blog / YouTube channel] about [your niche] for [your specific audience, e.g. "first-time landlords in the Midwest"]. I publish [once a week / twice a month]. Create a 12-week content calendar. For each week, give me: a working title, the one core idea in a single sentence, the format (how-to, story, opinion, breakdown, Q&A), and one reason my specific audience would care. Mix the formats across the 12 weeks. Avoid generic topics I could find on any blog — every idea should use the fact that I [your background/experience]. End with the 3 topics most likely to attract new subscribers and why.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
