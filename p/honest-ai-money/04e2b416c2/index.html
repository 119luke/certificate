I create content about [your niche] for [your audience]. Here are my last 5 pieces and how they performed: [list titles + what happened — views, replies, shares, crickets]. For each idea, flag whether it's aimed at attracting NEW audience or deepening trust with EXISTING audience. Be honest if the pattern suggests I should change direction.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
