The constraint sharpener

Chapter 3: Prompt Engineering That Actually Earns · The Honest AI Money Guide

Here is a prompt I use for client work: [paste your prompt]. Rewrite it with real constraints in four buckets: 1. AUDIENCE — make it specific (age, situation, knowledge level), not "beginners" 2. TONE — give 2-3 tone words plus negative constraints (words/phrases to never use) 3. LENGTH AND STRUCTURE — exact word counts and section structure 4. FORMAT — the exact shape the output should take (table, email, post, etc.) Keep my original intent. Show me the before and after, and explain in one line per bucket why the constraint improves the output.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
