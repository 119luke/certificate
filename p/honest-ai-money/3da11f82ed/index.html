The brief builder

Chapter 4: The Freelancer's AI Workflow · The Honest AI Money Guide

A client wants [deliverable] but their instructions are vague: "[paste what they said]". Generate exactly 5 clarifying questions I should send them — the fewest questions that would prevent the most revision rounds. For each question, explain in one line why the answer changes the work. Then draft the short email I'd send with these questions, under 120 words, friendly and professional.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
