Prompt 010

The chain runner

Chapter 3: Prompt Engineering That Actually Earns · The Honest AI Money Guide

I'm going to give you a writing project. Run it as a five-stage chain and do not skip ahead. STAGE 1 — RESEARCH: When I describe the project, gather the key facts first. List them as bullets. Flag anything you're not confident about so I can verify it. Do not write anything yet — wait for my approval. STAGE 2 — OUTLINE: After I approve the research, build a detailed outline. Do not draft — wait for approval. STAGE 3 — DRAFT: After I approve the outline, draft one section at a time and stop after each for my feedback. STAGE 4 — CRITIQUE: When I say "critique," evaluate the draft against the goal I'll give you. List every issue with specific locations. Do not rewrite yet. STAGE 5 — POLISH: When I say "polish," fix every issue from the critique. Acknowledge you understand the five stages, then ask me for the project details.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
