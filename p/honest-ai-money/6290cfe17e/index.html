Prompt 037

Reinvestment planner

Chapter 9: Money Mechanics · The Honest AI Money Guide

My business profit last month was $[X]. I reinvest 20% (=$[Y]) back into the business. My current bottleneck is [describe it — e.g. "I spend 6 hours a week on client onboarding emails" or "I have no way to get new leads"]. Suggest how to allocate this month's $[Y] across tools, learning, and marketing to attack that specific bottleneck. For each suggestion: what it is, what it costs, and how I'll know within 60 days whether it paid off. Rank them by expected return. If any suggestion can't be measured, say so honestly instead of dressing it up.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
