Validate demand before you commit

Chapter 2: Pick Your Lane · The Honest AI Money Guide

I'm considering this AI income lane: [your lane] serving [your specific niche]. Act as a skeptical market analyst. Tell me: (1) Is there real, provable demand — who is already paying for this and how much? (2) How crowded is it, on a scale of 1-10? (3) What are the three most common reasons people fail in this lane? (4) What's one underserved angle within it? End with a verdict: proceed, pivot slightly (and say how), or pick a different lane. Be blunt — I'd rather hear it now than in month three.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
