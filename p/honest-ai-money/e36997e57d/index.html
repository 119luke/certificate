Product validation

Chapter 6: Digital Products & Courses · The Honest AI Money Guide

I want to validate a digital product idea before I build it. My idea: [describe it in 2-3 sentences]. My target buyer: [describe the specific person]. Act as a skeptical product strategist. Tell me: (1) the painful, specific moment this fixes — or tell me if there isn't one, (2) three reasons it might fail, ranked by likelihood, (3) what a buyer would compare it against (including free alternatives), and (4) one change that would make it 10x more specific. Be blunt. If the idea is weak, say so and suggest one stronger variation.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
