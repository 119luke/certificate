Chapter 7: AI + Your Day Job · The Honest AI Money Guide

I want to find the highest-leverage places to use AI in my current job. My role is: [your job title]. My main responsibilities are: [list 5-8 things you actually do each week]. For each responsibility, tell me: (1) how much of it is repetitive vs. judgment-based, (2) specifically how AI could help with the repetitive parts, (3) what I should NOT let AI touch and why, and (4) a rough estimate of hours I could save per week. Rank the list by total value (time saved × visibility to my manager). Be specific to my role, not generic.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
