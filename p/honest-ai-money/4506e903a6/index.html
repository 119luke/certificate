Your skills-to-income fit

Chapter 1: The Honest Math · The Honest AI Money Guide

I want an honest assessment of which AI income lane fits me best. Here is my background: [describe your job history, skills, and interests in 3-5 sentences]. Here is my situation: I can commit [X] hours per week, I need my first income within [timeframe], and I am [comfortable / uncomfortable] with direct sales. The four lanes I'm considering are: (1) freelancing with AI, (2) content creation, (3) digital products, (4) AI services for local businesses. For each lane, give me: a fit score from 1-10 for my situation, the biggest advantage I'd have, the biggest obstacle I'd face, and one concrete first step. Then recommend the single best lane for me and explain why in two sentences. Be direct — don't flatter me.

Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or whichever assistant you use.
