The niche stress-test

Chapter 5: The Content Business · The Honest AI Money Guide

I'm considering building content around this niche: [describe it]. Stress-test it for me as a skeptical business analyst: (1) Is there evidence real people spend money here? Name the kinds of products and services they buy. (2) How crowded is it — who are the 3-5 established voices, and what are they missing? (3) Can a beginner with AI assistance realistically produce content as good as or better than what's out there, and where would the human edge come from? (4) What's the most likely way this niche fails? Give me a verdict: pursue, narrow further, or abandon — with reasons.
