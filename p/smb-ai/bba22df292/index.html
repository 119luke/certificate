How to make a short promo video from a description or a photo

Marketing, Content and Social Media · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Gemini · 2. Grok Bot · 3. Claude

Using the attached photo of [product or space], create a short [tall / square] video with a slow camera move and [mood, e.g. warm morning light]. Keep the product, colors and any logo exactly as they are. Then write three short captions for [platform] to go with it.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
