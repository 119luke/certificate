How to get a one-page brief before every call

Find and Win New Customers · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Claude · 3. Dots

Every weekday at 7 AM, look at today's calendar and, for each call with a customer or prospect, write a one-page brief: who they are, our history, the last three emails, anything I promised, open questions and a suggested goal for the call. For new prospects, add a short summary of their business from their website. Send me the briefs in one message.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
