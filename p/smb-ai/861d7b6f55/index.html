How to turn happy customers into testimonials and case studies

Reviews, Reputation and Local Search · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Claude · 2. Grok Bot · 3. Gemini

Write three friendly questions I can ask a happy customer for a testimonial. Then turn their answers into a 40-word testimonial and a one-page case study (problem, what we did, result) using only what they said, without inventing details: [paste their answers]. Draft a short note asking them to approve the wording.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
