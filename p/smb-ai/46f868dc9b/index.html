How to reply to every review within a day

Reviews, Reputation and Local Search · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Claude · 3. Gemini

Every morning, gather my new reviews from [Google, Yelp, Facebook]. Draft a short, warm reply to each in my voice that thanks the customer by name and mentions something specific they said. For anything three stars or below, draft a calm reply and flag it for me personally. Never include private details, and don't post anything.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
