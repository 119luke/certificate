How to create a welcome packet for new clients

Customer Service and Scheduling · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Claude · 2. Gemini · 3. Grok Bot

Create a welcome packet for new clients of my [type of business]. Include a warm thank-you, what happens next with timelines, what I need from them, how to reach me, how payments work, what to expect on the day and answers to common first-week worries. Add a short preparation checklist, make it a branded PDF, and write a short welcome email to send with it.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
