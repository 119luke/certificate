How to track supplier price changes

Getting Paid, Cash and Bookkeeping · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Dots · 3. Gemini

Each month, read my supplier invoices from [email folder / attached files] and update a price list of the unit prices I pay for my top 30 items. Flag any increase with the percentage and the yearly cost at my usual volume. For increases over [5]%, draft a polite message to the supplier and suggest two alternatives to compare. Don't send anything.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
