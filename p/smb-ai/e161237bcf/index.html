Job 22

How to write cold emails that get replies

Find and Win New Customers · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Claude · 2. Grok Bot · 3. Gemini

Research [business name and website] and find one real, recent detail I could mention. Then write a cold email of no more than five sentences: one line showing I did my homework, one line on how I help businesses like theirs [my service and a result], and one easy question. Use an honest subject line, add my business address and an opt-out line, and don't send it.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
