How to catch every promise and follow-up

Your Time, Admin and Paperwork · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Dots · 3. Claude

Read my emails, messages and meeting notes from the past [two weeks] and find every promise I or my team made to customers or suppliers. List each one with who promised what, to whom, by when, and a link to where it was said. Every morning, show me what's due and overdue, with a draft reply to close each one.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
