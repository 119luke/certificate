Job 113

How to brief, track and pay contractors cleanly

Cut Costs and Manage Suppliers · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Claude · 2. Grok Bot · 3. Dots

Write a clear brief for a [contractor type] to [project]: deliverables, deadline [date], budget [amount], how I'll judge the result and payment terms. Then set up a tracking sheet for all my contractors with projects, deadlines, invoices and payment status, and remind me two days before each deadline and in December about W-9s and year-end forms.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
