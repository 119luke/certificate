How to send progress updates with photos

Trades, Shops and Local Services · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Gemini · 3. Claude

I'll send you a photo and a short note at key stages of the [customer] job. Turn each into a friendly update: what was done, what's next, anything they need to decide, and when we're back. Keep it under 80 words with the photo. Flag anything that changes the price or needs their decision, and keep every update as a draft for me.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
