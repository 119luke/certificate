How to build a compliance checklist for your state

Hiring, Training and Your Team · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Claude · 2. Gemini · 3. Dots

Build an employment compliance checklist for a [type of business] in [city, state] with [number] employees. Using official federal, state and city government sources, list each requirement that likely applies (wages, overtime, posters, sick leave, breaks, new-hire reporting, workers' comp, training), what I need to do, how often, and a link to the official page. Flag rules that change as I grow. This is general information, not legal advice.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
