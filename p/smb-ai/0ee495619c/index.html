How to turn one event into ten real leads

Find and Win New Customers · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Claude · 3. Gemini

I'm going to [event name, date, link]. Research the exhibitors and speakers and list the 10 who are most likely to need [my service], with one line on why. Write me a 20-second pitch and three questions that get people talking about their problems. After the event, I'll send card photos and notes; draft a personal follow-up for each, saved as drafts.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
