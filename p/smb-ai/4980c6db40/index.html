How to find and pitch local creators and influencers

Marketing, Content and Social Media · The Best AI for Small Business Owners

Best AI for this job: 1. Grok Bot · 2. Gemini · 3. Claude

Find 15 creators in [area] who post about [topics my customers care about] on [platforms]. For each, note followers, typical engagement, what they post and any past local partnerships. Rank them by fit for my [type of business], and draft a short, friendly collaboration offer for the top five that asks for an honest post with clear #ad disclosure.

Paste it into Claude, Gemini, Grok Bot, Dots or Muse, then swap the [brackets] for your own details.
