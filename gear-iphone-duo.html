Tip 31

TV and monitor connections

iPhone Duo outputs up to 4K HDR over USB-C. Use a USB-C to HDMI adapter (Apple's is the USB-C Digital AV Multiport Adapter) for a TV, or a USB-C display cable for a monitor that accepts video over USB-C. VGA is only for older projectors and can't carry HDR.
